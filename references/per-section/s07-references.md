## Key Synthesis Points

| Domain | Theory Status | Materials Status | Gap |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|-----|
| Spacetime distortion | Mathematical metrics exist | Source formation, stability, conservation and control unresolved | No universal fabrication factor |
| Negative stress-energy | Ideal QFT configurations exist | No freely configurable macroscopic source | No universal Casimir-to-warp ratio |
| Fusion energy | Reaction physics established | No compact mobile net-electric reactor | Not a demonstrated warp power chain |
| Structural materials | CNT properties measured | Relevant UAP inertial/pressure/tidal load is unmeasured | No defensible UAP strength factor |
| Metamaterial cloaking | Microwave demonstrated | Optical losses | Qualitative |
| Target stress-energy control | GR already couples stress-energy to curvature | Required source configuration not demonstrated | Foundational/model-dependent |

## Notes

- A mathematical metric is not evidence that its required stress-energy is physically realizable.
- GR requires no separate energy-to-curvature interaction; the missing task is source realization, formation, control, and stability.
- Semiconductor and superconductor histories do not establish that energy-condition or quantum-inequality constraints are engineerable.
