## Definitive Evidence Criteria

1. Preregistered metric-specific predictions of kinematics, curvature, matter coupling, and any radiation
2. Repeatable calibrated measurements above stated detector sensitivity and backgrounds
3. A characterized source whose stress-energy and dynamics generate the predicted geometry
4. Independent replication with raw data, metadata, uncertainty propagation, and conventional alternatives tested
5. Confirmed acceleration >1,000 g by multiple calibrated sensors
6. Confirmed transmedium transition observed from both sides
7. Material recovery with non-terrestrial manufacturing signatures

## Notes

- Synchronized calibrated multi-sensor collection with full metadata is the highest priority; THz is one optional band, not a privileged warp signature.
- Analog-gravity systems test their stated analog dynamics, not Einstein-equation sourcing or propulsion.
- Modified gravity theories (f(R), scalar-tensor) may relax warp energy conditions — active research area.
