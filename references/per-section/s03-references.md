## Notes

- The Santiago–Schuster–Visser (2022) rebuttal of Lentz (2021) and Fell–Heisenberg (2021) is significant: it showed that checking energy conditions only for co-moving Eulerian observers is insufficient.
- Bobrick & Martire construct a continuous family of material-shell metrics; their Earth-mass/10 m example is not a universal lower bound and the shell still requires propulsion.
- A generic Alcubierre metric does not provide an impermeable matter boundary. Matter interaction and apparent morphology require explicit particle/null-geodesic calculations.
- The ER=EPR conjecture remains unproven and provides no pathway to macroscopic traversable wormholes.
