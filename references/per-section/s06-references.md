## Notes

- "Art's Parts" specimen: ORNL assessed terrestrial manufacture with high confidence and a bismuth THz-waveguide function as highly unlikely (synopsis April 2024); AARO's July 2024 supplement assesses it as likely a mid-20th-century Mg-alloy test object, manufacturing product or byproduct. Neither document identifies it as Betterton–Kroll dross; chain of custody and original purpose remain unresolved.
- Macroscopic strong-force-bonded materials are speculative with no known physical mechanism for extending strong interactions beyond ~10⁻¹⁵ m.
- Optical-frequency metamaterial cloaking faces qualitative (not just quantitative) barriers: absorption loss near resonance, and passivity/causality bounds on the bandwidth and scattering reduction of any passive cloak that tighten as the object grows relative to the wavelength (src-070).
